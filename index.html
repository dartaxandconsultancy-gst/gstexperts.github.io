Latest GST Changes

57th GST Council meeting, 8 October 2026. These are recommendations; check the relevant notifications and effective dates before acting.

Swipe to read the updates
GST
GST registration

Clearer application requirements

The Council recommended clearer document guidance and simpler registration processes. This could help businesses prepare applications with fewer queries.

8 October 2026 · Recommendation
GST
GST refunds

Faster refund processing proposed

More automated refund processing was recommended. The aim is to reduce delays and improve business cash flow, subject to the applicable rules and conditions.

8 October 2026 · Recommendation
GST
Input tax credit

Changes to eligible tax credit

The Council recommended widening input tax credit eligibility. Businesses should check the final amendments before changing their claims.

8 October 2026 · Recommendation
GST
Small businesses

A simpler filing scheme proposed

An optional annual-return and quarterly-payment scheme received in-principle approval for eligible consumer-facing businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore. It is not an automatic change to your filing obligations.

8 October 2026 · Recommendation
