Review & Rating Policy

Updated 16 October 2026.

Who can submit

Adults with a signed-in account may submit one review about a genuine experience with DAR Tax and Consultancy Services. Choose a display name, give a rating from 1 to 5, and describe your experience in your own words.

Approval before publication

Submissions are private until reviewed by our team. We check for relevance, spam, impersonation, unlawful content and personal or confidential information. Positive and negative feedback are both eligible for publication; a low rating alone is not a reason to reject a review. We do not publish fabricated reviews or change a customer's rating.

What becomes public

With your consent, approved reviews display your chosen name, rating, review text and publication date. Your sign-in email and account identifier are not included in the public review record. Please do not include tax IDs, phone numbers, financial data or confidential case details.

Ratings and counts

The displayed average is the arithmetic mean of published star ratings, rounded to one decimal place. The review count is the number of published reviews, not the firm's total clients, website visitors or successful cases. Pending and rejected submissions are excluded. Ratings can change when reviews are published or removed.

Correction, withdrawal and complaints

To correct or withdraw your review, or report a concern about another review, contact dartaxandconsultancy@gmail.com or +91 9596171766. We may verify ownership before making changes. We do not promise a specific approval time.
