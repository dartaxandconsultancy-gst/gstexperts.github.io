Privacy & account information

DAR Tax and Consultancy Services uses Google Firebase to authenticate client accounts. Google sign-in or email/password sign-in may provide your account ID, email address, verification status and, where available, profile name and image.

How we use account information

We use your account to provide access to service information and protect that access. Password authentication is handled by Firebase; our team does not need your password. Firebase processes authentication and technical data under its applicable terms and privacy information.

Documents and enquiries

This website provides preparation checklists. It does not accept document uploads. WhatsApp and email buttons open external services, governed by their own privacy practices. Contact our team to agree how to provide supporting records.

Account questions

For account deletion or privacy requests, email dartaxandconsultancy@gmail.com. Account verification may be needed to process a request. Signing out removes the website's active session on this browser.
