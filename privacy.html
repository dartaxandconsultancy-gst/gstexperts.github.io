Privacy Policy

Updated 17 October 2026. This policy explains how DAR Tax and Consultancy Services handles information through gstexperts.org.

Who to contact

Paul Complex, Opposite R & B Department, Chadoora. For privacy questions, corrections, withdrawal of review consent, account deletion or complaints, email dartaxandconsultancy@gmail.com or call +91 9596171766.

Accounts and access

Google Firebase processes sign-in information, including your account identifier, email address and, where supplied, profile name and image. Public service overviews do not require sign-in. We use authentication to associate feedback with your account and for any existing restricted service checklists. Password handling is provided by Firebase; never send us your password or OTP.

Reviews and your choice

When you submit feedback, we store your account identifier, chosen display name, star rating, review, submission time and publication consent. New reviews publish immediately after successful submission. Your display name, rating, review and publication time are public; ownership records remain private. Older unpublished submissions are not automatically made public. We do not publish your account identifier or email in the review card.

Public reviews may be copied or indexed by others. You may ask us to remove your review or withdraw consent using the contact details above. We may verify account ownership before acting. Removing a review does not automatically remove copies made by third parties.

Enquiries and documents

The enquiry form prepares a message in WhatsApp; you choose whether to send it. Email and WhatsApp use their own privacy terms. This website does not accept tax-document uploads. Do not include PAN, Aadhaar, financial records, case identifiers or other confidential information in public feedback.

Hosting, storage and external services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Firebase for authentication and review storage. These providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure their services. The light/dark preference is saved in your browser; authentication uses browser session storage. We have not added advertising trackers or a separate visitor analytics service.

Choosing an external Google translation opens a public-page translation through Google. Translation services have their own privacy practices. Sign in and submit forms on the original gstexperts.org website.

Retention and requests

Account and feedback records are retained while needed for their stated purposes, handling requests, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion. If a legal obligation requires retention, we will explain the applicable limitation. This website's feedback and account features are intended for adults aged 18 or over.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website changes and will revise the date above. Material changes affecting publication consent will be explained before seeking new consent.
