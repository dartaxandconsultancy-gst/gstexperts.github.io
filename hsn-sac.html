HSN & SAC codes
and GST rates

HSN classifies goods. SAC classifies services. A code alone may not determine the tax rate: the description, exemptions, conditions and date of supply matter.

Codes and descriptions are from the GST spreadsheet supplied by DAR Tax. GST rates are not included in that file. Rate verification is pending; this is not a current tax-rate calculator.
